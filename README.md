> **godown** *(noun)*: a warehouse.

Godown is a B2B procurement platform for manufacturing companies. It covers everything from "the plant needs this part" to after-sales support: sourcing, quotes, purchase orders, deliveries, billing and returns, all in one place.

Industrial buying still runs mostly on phone calls, spreadsheets and long email threads. A single request for a spare part can pass through several approvers and many suppliers before anything ships. Godown puts that whole chain on one platform. Buyers get competitive prices faster, suppliers get steady demand that fits what they sell, and everyone can see where an order stands.

## Who we work with

- **Manufacturers.** Plants and stores that need industrial materials, delivered on time and at the right price.
- **Vendors.** Stockists and authorised distributors who want more orders from industrial buyers.
- **Partners.** People who bring new vendors to Godown and earn rewards for it.

## How it works

Tell us what you need. We find the right supplier at a fair price and look after the order from start to finish, including any help you need after delivery.
