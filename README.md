> **godown** *(noun)*: a warehouse.

Godown is a B2B procurement platform for manufacturing companies. It covers everything between "the plant needs this part" and "the vendor has been paid": sourcing, quotes, purchase orders, deliveries, billing and returns, all in one place.

Industrial buying still runs mostly on phone calls, spreadsheets and long email threads. A single request for a spare part can pass through several approvers and many suppliers before anything ships. Godown puts that whole chain on one platform. Buyers get competitive prices faster, suppliers get steady demand that fits what they sell, and everyone can see where an order stands.

## Who we work with

- **Manufacturers.** Plants and stores that raise material requests, approve spending and track orders.
- **Vendors.** Stockists and authorised distributors who receive requests for quotes, send prices and fulfil orders.
- **Partners.** People who bring new vendors to Godown and earn rewards when those vendors win orders.

## How it works

1. **Request.** A plant raises an indent (a material request), and it moves through the company's approvals.
2. **Source.** Godown sends the request to vendors who supply that kind of item and collects competing quotes.
3. **Order.** The buyer picks a quote and a purchase order goes out, synced to the company's ERP where needed.
4. **Deliver.** The vendor dispatches the goods and the plant records what arrived.
5. **Settle.** Invoices, payments and returns are tracked through to the end.
